# Self

David Ungar and Randall B. Smith, [“Self: The Power of Simplicity”](https://bibliography.selflanguage.org/self-power.html), *OOPSLA 1987*.

Self influenced the Llull-01 exploration of slots, delegation, direct-return and method slots, and message syntax. This citation is retained with the archived Llull-01 material because that design branch is historically important to the development of the project, but Self is not presently a technical influence on the active Llull-02 work.
